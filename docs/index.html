Schedule Risk Tool

A schedule and cost simulator for Indian building projects. It shows the range of likely finish dates and costs, not just one.

Open the app

Why simulate?

A single critical path gives one finish date, and it is usually too optimistic. Real projects have uncertain durations, risks such as monsoon delays, and activities that slip together. The tool runs the project thousands of times and reports how often each outcome happens.

What it does

Schedule simulation

PERT-Beta durations and a forward pass across every iteration at once.

Correlated delays

Links activities that tend to slip together, such as outdoor work in the monsoon.

Risk register

Weighted discrete risks with delay and cost impacts, switchable on or off.

Cost and finance

Cost P80, contingency, and NPV and IRR distributions.

Mitigation calculator

Compares the cost of a fix with the delay cost it avoids.

Validated

Checked against a hand-calculated benchmark and an Excel model.

How it works

  1. Enter activities with optimistic, most likely and pessimistic durations, and their predecessors.
  2. Add risks and correlation groups.
  3. Run 10,000 iterations.
  4. Read P50, P80 and P90, the criticality index, and the contingency against a flat 10%.
